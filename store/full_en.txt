Quran & Science: Proofs shows Quran verses beside the discoveries scientists made centuries later, and how each one was actually proven.

56 facts, each with:
• The verse in Arabic, with a translation in your language (English, Russian, Uzbek and more) and Alafasy recitation
• What the verse says first, then the science
• A proof timeline: who measured or discovered it, when, and the original evidence (photographs, plots, recordings)
• "Where it fits" and "Where to be careful": an honest look at how strong each link is
• Photos, video and audio from NASA, ESA, ESO, NOAA, Wikimedia Commons and others

HONEST BY DESIGN
Every fact is labelled by how strong the link is: Parallel, Interpretive, Historical or Miracle (faith, not science). Debated claims are marked as debated, and viral stories that do not hold up are rejected, so you can judge for yourself.
