Quran & Science: Proofs shows Quran verses beside the scientific discoveries made centuries later, and how each one was actually proven.

56 FACTS, EACH WITH
• The verse in Arabic, a translation in your language and Alafasy recitation
• What the verse says first, then what science found
• A proof timeline: who measured or discovered it, when, and the original evidence (photographs, plots, recordings)
• "Where it fits" and "Where to be careful": an honest look at how strong each link is
• Photos, video and audio from NASA, ESA, ESO, NOAA, Wikimedia Commons and others

HONEST BY DESIGN
Every fact carries a label: Scientific parallel, Interpretive reading, Historical sign or Miracle (faith, not science). Debated claims are marked as debated and viral stories that do not hold up are left out.

TOPICS
Scientific miracles of the Quran: the Big Bang and the expanding universe, orbits of the Sun and Moon, mountains, the two seas, the deep sea, embryo development, iron from space, the water cycle, bees and ants, fingerprints, ancient history and archaeology, the oldest Quran manuscripts and more.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
The whole app, the facts and the verse translations are available in English, Uzbek, Russian, Turkish, French, Indonesian, Urdu, Bengali and German. Verses always come from published translations by named translators; we never translate verses ourselves.
